Our Story

Beauty With Purpose

Glow Haven was founded on the belief that beauty is more than appearance — it is confidence, wellness and self-expression.

Based in Gaborone, Botswana, we serve a vibrant community of beauty lovers who value high-quality skincare and beauty services.

Our team combines expertise in facials, makeup, nails and skincare consultation — all under one roof.
